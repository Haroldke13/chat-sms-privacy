Chat SMS: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Chat SMS (“the app”) is an SMS/MMS messaging, phone (dialer), contacts and calendar app for Android, published by Joel Harold Onyango (H0J-MQH Holdings Limited). This policy explains what the app accesses, why, and where that data goes.

Summary: the app has no server and no accounts. It does not show ads, use analytics, or sell or share your data. Everything stays on your device unless you choose one of the optional features in section 3.

1. Data the app accesses and why

Data Permission Why Leaves the device?
SMS and MMS messages READ_SMS, RECEIVE_SMS, RECEIVE_MMS, SEND_SMS As your default SMS app: show conversations, send messages you write or schedule, and notify you of new messages Only through your mobile carrier, as normal SMS/MMS
Call history READ_CALL_LOG, WRITE_CALL_LOG As your default phone app: show the Recents tab, call details, and delete entries you choose No
Phone calls CALL_PHONE, USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT Place calls you start; show the incoming-call screen, including over the lock screen No
Contacts READ_CONTACTS, WRITE_CONTACTS Name your chats and calls; add, edit, favourite, block or delete a contact when you ask No
Calendar READ_CALENDAR, WRITE_CALENDAR Show and edit your calendar events in the Calendar tab No (your own calendar account syncs as usual)
SIM information READ_PHONE_STATE List your SIM cards so you can choose which one sends or calls No
Notifications, reboot, alarms POST_NOTIFICATIONS, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED, SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM Message and call alerts; keep scheduled messages and reminders after a restart; ring reminders on time No
Fingerprint / face USE_BIOMETRIC Optional app lock No (Android handles it; the app never sees biometric data)

The app does not record calls and does not record or store audio. Voice dictation uses Android’s system speech service, which is covered by that service’s own policy.

2. Data stored on your device

  • Messages, contacts, call history and calendar events stay in Android’s own system stores.
  • Reminders, journal entries, notes, settings, any AI API key you enter, and a local security log are stored in the app’s private storage, encrypted with AES-256-GCM.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes this data. You can also delete it at any time in Android Settings → Apps → Chat SMS → Storage → Clear data.

3. Optional features that send data off the device

Nothing below happens unless you turn it on or tap it.

  • AI insights. When you tap ✨ and confirm, the text of the conversation that is open is sent over HTTPS to the AI provider you chose (Google Gemini or OpenAI), using an API key you supplied. That provider’s privacy policy applies to the request. Nothing is sent automatically or in the background.
  • Ask another AI app. This hands text you select to an AI app or website you pick (for example Claude, ChatGPT, DeepSeek or Gemini) through Android’s normal share/open mechanism.
  • Security alerts by e-mail. If you enter your own e-mail address and mail account in Settings, the app e-mails you high-severity security events, such as another app taking over SMS, or a SIM change. They go only to the address you set.
  • Sharing and WhatsApp. When you share something, or choose “Reply on WhatsApp”, the text goes to the app you picked.

4. What the app does not do

  • No advertising and no advertising IDs.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • No sale, rental or sharing of personal data.
  • No accounts, and no server of our own.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

6. Security

Data at rest is encrypted as described in section 2. Data in transit for the optional features in section 3 uses HTTPS or TLS.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at the same address with a new “Last updated” date. Material changes are also shown in the app.
