Chat SMS: Data Safety

Last updated: 8 November 2026 · app version 1.0.0

This page sets out what data Chat SMS collects and shares, matching its Google Play Data safety section. See the full privacy policy for details.

At a glance

Data collected by the developer None. The app has no server and no accounts.
Data shared with third parties Only when you ask: the text of the open conversation goes to the AI provider you chose, when you tap ✨ and confirm
Ads or tracking None
Data encrypted in transit Yes (HTTPS / TLS)
Data encrypted on the device Yes (AES-256-GCM for app data)
You can delete your data Yes: in the app, via Settings → Apps → Chat SMS → Clear data, or by uninstalling

Data that never leaves your phone

Data Used for
SMS and MMS messages Showing, sending and notifying you about your messages (sent only through your carrier)
Call history The Recents tab and deleting entries you choose
Contacts Naming chats and calls; adding or editing contacts when you ask
Calendar events The Calendar tab (your own calendar account syncs as usual)
Reminders, journal, notes Stored encrypted on the device only
SIM information Choosing which SIM sends or calls
Fingerprint / face Optional app lock, handled by Android. The app never receives biometric data

Chat SMS does not record calls or audio, and does not use location, photos, files, financial or health data.

Optional features that send data

Feature What is sent To whom When
AI insights Text of the conversation that is open Google Gemini or OpenAI, whichever you chose, using your own API key Only when you tap ✨ and confirm
Ask another AI app Text you select The AI app or website you pick Only when you choose it
Security e-mail alerts High-severity security events, such as a change of SIM or of the default SMS app Your own e-mail address Only if you set up your own mail account
Share / WhatsApp reply The text you share The app you pick Only when you share

Data sent to an AI provider is covered by that provider’s privacy policy. The developer never receives a copy.
